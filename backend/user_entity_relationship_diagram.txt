=====================================================================================
                    OFFERWALA — ENTITY RELATIONSHIP DIAGRAM (PostgreSQL)
=====================================================================================

  LEGEND
    PK  = primary key            1 ──< *   = one-to-many
    FK  = foreign key            * >──< *  = many-to-many (through a link table)
    UQ  = unique                 every main table also has created_at, updated_at


                                     THE BIG PICTURE
                                     ───────────────

        ┌─────────┐   1 ──< *   ┌─────────┐
        │  BANKS  │────────────>│  CARDS  │
        └─────────┘             └─────────┘
             │ 1                     │ 1
             │                       │
             │ *                     │ *
             v              ┌────────────────┐
        ┌─────────┐   1 ──< │  offer_cards   │   (per-card discount)
        │ OFFERS  │────────>└────────────────┘
        └─────────┘
          │ 1   │ 1
          │     │          ┌────────────────────┐  * >── 1  ┌──────────────┐
          │     └─────────>│  offer_businesses  │──────────>│  BUSINESSES  │
          │          1──<* └────────────────────┘           └──────────────┘
          │                                                  (one location each)
          │          1──<* ┌────────────────────────┐ * >── 1 ┌─────────┐
          └───────────────>│  user_favourite_offers │────────>│  USERS  │
                           └────────────────────────┘         └─────────┘

     In words:
       • One BANK has many CARDS            (Platinum, Gold, Silver ...)
       • One BANK publishes many OFFERS
       • OFFERS  <──>  CARDS       many-to-many through offer_cards
                                   (each card can have its OWN discount on the same offer)
       • OFFERS  <──>  BUSINESSES  many-to-many through offer_businesses
                                   (one offer at many branches / restaurants)
       • A BUSINESS accepts many cards  → found through its offers
       • A BUSINESS has ONE location     (address, city, latitude, longitude)
       • USERS   <──>  OFFERS      many-to-many through user_favourite_offers (favourites)

=====================================================================================
                          EXAMPLE: HOW ONE SCRAPED DEAL IS STORED
=====================================================================================

   banks          MCB Bank
     └─ cards        MCB Platinum Debit, MCB Gold Credit ...
     └─ offers       "30% off on Every Saturday"   valid_days=[Sat]  valid_till=2026-12-31
          ├─ offer_cards        Platinum → 30%,  Gold → 30%  ...
          └─ offer_businesses   Cafe Zouk (Gulberg, Lahore, lat 31.5, lng 74.3)

   Deleting a bank deletes its cards and offers (CASCADE); deleting an offer
   removes its rows in offer_cards, offer_businesses and user_favourite_offers.
